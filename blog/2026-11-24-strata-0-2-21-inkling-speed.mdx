Strata 0.2.21 shipped on 24 Nov, updating the app, Terminal, and all three engine families (strata-engine, strata-inkling, strata-hy4) plus Control to 0.1.67. The standout change: Inkling 2 Mini is much faster on most GPUs, and agent sessions now reuse their prompt on every backend.

## Releases

- **Strata 0.2.21** (24 Nov): app and Terminal are 0.2.21; all engine families and Control move to 0.1.67 on every supported platform and backend. Inkling 2 Mini is much faster on most cards versus 0.2.20: on an RTX 4090 after a 32K prompt it reaches 92 tok/s instead of 29 (UD-Q4_K_XL), an RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB gets 43 instead of 11 tok/s (SQ-10), and an RTX 5070 12 GB 68 instead of 12.5. Prompt reading also improved, e.g. RTX 5090 at 6,967 instead of 3,923 tok/s. Agent sessions now reuse their prompt on every backend, and the DSpark drafter works with Unsloth's re-uploaded GGUF files. Source: https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/releases/tag/v0.2.21
