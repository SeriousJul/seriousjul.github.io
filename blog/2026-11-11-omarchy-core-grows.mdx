Omarchy announced major growth on its people side this month. Marcelo Alcantara and Birk Skyum, credited as two of the people doing the most to take Omarchy beyond x86, joined Omarchy Core on November 5. The Omacom Foundation also added Greptile as a Distinguished Corporate Patron (October 15) and hired HANCORE as Head of Design and Community (October 27).

## Community

- On November 5, 2026, Omarchy announced that Marcelo Alcantara and Birk Skyum are joining Omarchy Core. Alcantara coordinated the Omarchy M effort, a sixteen-person team that shipped the native macOS installer in Omarchy 4.5, letting Omarchy run alongside macOS on Apple Silicon Macs; Skyum's work on Omarchy Dragon is also highlighted in the post. Source: https://omarchy.org/news/2026/11/marcelo-alcantara-and-birk-skyum-join-omarchy-core/
- On October 15, 2026, the Omacom Foundation announced Greptile as a Distinguished Corporate Patron, contributing $150,000 in tokens for its PR review service so every pull request coming into Omarchy can be reviewed. The post notes Greptile has already reviewed almost 1,500 pull requests, and that across the 60 most recently merged PRs it reviewed, it flagged 39 issues it rated as serious, including a Turkish-locale lowercasing bug in laptop model matching. Source: https://omarchy.org/news/2026/10/greptile-joins-as-a-distinguished-corporate-patron/
- On October 27, 2026, the Omacom Foundation announced it is hiring HANCORE as Head of Design and Community, making him its fourth staff member alongside Krzysztof on Kernel, outfoxxed on Shell, and Emir on Infrastructure. He moves from his Omarchy AIR residency seat, where he started the plugin marketplace that grew into the official plugin catalogue with six thousand plugins, and whose themes (Solitude and Last Horizon) ship with Omarchy. Source: https://omarchy.org/news/2026/10/omacom-foundation-hires-hancore/
