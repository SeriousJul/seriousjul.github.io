On Nov 20 the Omacom Foundation announced it will sponsor Aurora Silicon, the independent open-source project bringing Linux to Apple Silicon Macs, providing hardware, tokens, and direct funding to its contributors. The same day Beelink joined the Omacom Foundation as a Distinguished Corporate Patron, pledging $100,000 a year for the next three years. A community weekly roundup followed on Nov 21.
