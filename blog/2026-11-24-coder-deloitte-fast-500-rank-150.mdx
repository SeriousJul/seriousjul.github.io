Coder announced on 2026-11-18 that it ranked number 150 on the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500, the list of North America's fastest-growing technology companies, citing 477% revenue growth from 2022 to 2025. The release highlights enterprises standardizing on Coder's self-hosted infrastructure for AI-driven development and adds to its 2026 Inc. 5000 recognition.

## Community

- 2026-11-18: Coder ranked No. 150 on the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500 with 477% revenue growth (2022-2025), per its [official announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/18/3405865/0/en/coder-ranks-no-150-on-the-2026-deloitte-technology-fast-500-with-477-revenue-growth.html). It notes organizations are standardizing on Coder's self-hosted infrastructure to scale AI-driven development, and that the ranking follows its Inc. 5000 and Inc. 5000 Regionals: Southwest placements.
