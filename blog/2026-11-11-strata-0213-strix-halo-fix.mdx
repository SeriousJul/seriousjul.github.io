Strata 0.2.13 (engine 0.1.59) shipped on November 9, 2026 with new per-card DFlash2 drafting defaults, a much faster app start, an Intel Arc determinism fix, a Linux expert-store loading fix, and groundwork for Qwen4. Press coverage highlights the Strix Halo Terminal launch fix, which cut startup from up to about 10 minutes to 60-85 seconds, and notes the project has passed 42,000 GitHub stars.

## Releases
- Strata 0.2.13 released on 2026-11-09 (app and Terminal 0.2.13, Control and all engine packages 0.1.59; DGX Spark also receives 0.2.13). The update brings new card-specific DFlash2 drafting defaults (49.1 tok/s on Strix Halo, +5.2%), a faster app start, and Qwen4 groundwork: [release notes](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/releases/tag/v0.2.13).

## Fixes and other
- The release includes a Strix Halo Terminal launch fix reducing startup from up to ~594 s to 60-85 s, an Intel Arc determinism fix, and a fix for broken Linux expert-store loading, plus follow-up 0.2.13.1 letting the Linux desktop app set up models on Intel Arc cards: [release notes](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/releases/tag/v0.2.13).

## Community
- Linux Compatible covered the 0.2.13 release on 2026-11-09, calling it the most practical update yet to the engine and noting Strata has climbed past 42,000 GitHub stars: [article](https://www.linuxcompatible.org/story/strata-0213-strix-halo-launch-fix-and-qwen4-prep-for-the-125b-consumerpc-llm-engine/).
