Actual Budget released version 26.11.0 on 2026-11-02, its latest stable release. The update adds automatic bank syncing at a chosen interval, custom reports grouped by tags, and a more customizable sidebar, alongside per-budget SimpleFIN credentials and performance optimizations for large budgets. It also introduces a breaking change: custom SimpleFIN bridges on private networks now require a server allowlist.

## Releases
- v26.11.0 released on 2026-11-02, adding automatic bank syncing at a chosen interval, custom reports grouped by tags, and more customizable sidebar navigation ([source](https://github.com/actualbudget/actual/releases/tag/v26.11.0)).
- The same release includes per-budget SimpleFIN credentials and performance optimizations for large budgets ([source](https://github.com/actualbudget/actual/releases/tag/v26.11.0)).

## Fixes and other
- Breaking change in v26.11.0 (2026-11-02): custom SimpleFIN bridges on private networks now require a server allowlist ([source](https://github.com/actualbudget/actual/releases/tag/v26.11.0)).
