OmniRoute shipped v3.8.54 on 2026-11-17, its biggest cycle yet with an official "Sign in with ChatGPT" provider, several new providers, endpoint-specific combo routes, per-key rolling 5-hour USD budgets, and an opt-in PII token vault. Days later the project also gained third-party attention with a Level Up feature article pitching it as the ultimate AI gateway for 2026.

## Releases
- v3.8.54 released 2026-11-17 (920 commits, 121 contributors in the cycle): official "Sign in with ChatGPT" provider (PKCE + live model discovery), MiniMax Code, RenderHour and other new providers, endpoint-specific combo routes with new jev and expiry-first strategies, per-key rolling 5-hour USD budgets, and an opt-in PII token vault. Docker images are now also published to the Cheaper Inference org (cheaperinference/omniroute) and GHCR. Source: https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/releases/tag/v3.8.54

## Community
- Hirusha Fernando published "Never Ending AI Coding With OmniRoute" on Level Up (2026-11-18), describing the single OpenAI-compatible endpoint, four-tier subscription-to-free fallback, and npm/Docker setup for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Cline and Copilot. Source: https://levelup.gitconnected.com/never-ending-ai-coding-with-omniroute-db418b681406
