llama.cpp and ggml have reached their first stable v1.0.0 releases on November 13, 2026, ending roughly three and a half years of sub-1.0 development. The team, which has been developing the project at Hugging Face since February 2026, committed to semantic versioning going forward: major version bumps will only occur for breaking API or ABI changes.

## Releases

- **llama.cpp v1.0.0 and ggml v1.0.0 (November 13, 2026):** ggml-org published the first stable 1.0.0 of llama.cpp (libllama) and its underlying ggml library. This marks the first 1.0.0 milestone for the project, which started in March 2023 as an inference engine for Meta's LLaMA model and now supports most open-source LLMs on a wide range of hardware. Future major version updates will be tied to compatibility-breaking API/ABI changes. Source: [Hugging Face blog, llama.cpp v1.0.0: A stronger foundation for the local AI ecosystem](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llama-cpp-v1)

## Community

- **Milestone covered by GIGAZINE (November 16, 2026):** GIGAZINE reported that llama.cpp and ggml have finally reached version 1.0.0. The article traces the project's history from its March 2023 release by Georgi Gerganov, through its 2026 move to Hugging Face, and notes its role as the underlying engine for popular local AI applications such as LM Studio and Unsloth Desktop. Source: [GIGAZINE, 'llama.cpp' has finally reached version 1.0.0](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20261116-llama-cpp-ggml-milestone/)
