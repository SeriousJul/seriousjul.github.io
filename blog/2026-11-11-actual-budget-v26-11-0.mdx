Actual Budget published its v26.11.0 stable release on November 2, 2026. The update brings automatic bank account syncing on a chosen interval, along with new reporting and customization options, plus performance improvements for large budgets.

## Releases
- v26.11.0 was released as the latest stable version on November 2, 2026. Highlights include automatic bank account syncing at a chosen interval, tag-based grouping for custom reports, customizable sidebar pages and start page, per-budget SimpleFIN credentials, and performance and memory optimizations for large budgets. ([source](https://github.com/actualbudget/actual/releases/tag/v26.11.0))
