OmniRoute shipped two stable releases in this window: v3.8.52 on 2026-10-19 and v3.8.53 on 2026-11-06, the latter now marked as the latest release. Both carried large documented changelogs with attached release notes and credited large contributor bases.

## Releases

- v3.8.53 (2026-11-06): shipped as a stable release and is currently the latest; it includes 1,189 documented changes from 1,162 commits, with complete notes attached as CHANGELOG-v3.8.53.md. Source: https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/releases/tag/v3.8.53
- v3.8.52 (2026-10-19): published as a stable release with 1,442 documented changes (135 features, 1,112 fixes, 195 maintenance entries) from 1,386 commits, with full notes attached as CHANGELOG-v3.8.52.md. Source: https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/releases/tag/v3.8.52

## Community

- The release pages for v3.8.52 (2026-10-19) and v3.8.53 (2026-11-06) thank their contributor bases: 306 external contributors for v3.8.52 and 222 people contributing to v3.8.53. Sources: https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/releases/tag/v3.8.52, https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/releases/tag/v3.8.53
